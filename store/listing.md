# Draft Chrome Web Store listing

Name: NoShorts

Summary: Less scrolling, more subscriptions. Hide YouTube Shorts and make Home open Subscriptions.

Description:
NoShorts does two small things: hides Shorts and makes YouTube Home take you straight to your subscriptions. Direct Shorts links open in the regular video player. No settings to fiddle with. No analytics. No data collection. Just a little pig in a jacket helping you skip the endless scroll.

Works on desktop YouTube. A YouTube sign-in is needed for subscriptions. Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## Before submission

- Test with a signed-in account: subscriptions grid/list, Home/logo clicks, Back/Forward, search, watch recommendations, channel tabs, History, and infinite scroll.
- Check the mascot and legibility in Chrome's toolbar and extension manager.
- Review the name's availability and branding before publication.
- Register a Chrome Web Store developer account and complete required verification/payment.
- Host PRIVACY.md at a publicly accessible URL and supply a support contact.
- Capture genuine product screenshots at the store's required dimensions; prepare promotional assets if required.
- Upload the zip produced by npm run package.
- Declare the single purpose: customize YouTube to remove Shorts discovery and default to Subscriptions.
- Explain content-script site access: YouTube-only URL redirects and CSS hiding. No extra API permissions requested.
- Complete privacy disclosures consistently with PRIVACY.md (no data collection, no remote code).
- Choose visibility/distribution and submit for review. Do not claim approval in advance.
