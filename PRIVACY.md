# NoShorts privacy policy

NoShorts does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, cookies, or external services.

The extension runs only on desktop YouTube pages at https://youtube.com and https://www.youtube.com. It checks the current page URL to redirect Home and Shorts links, and applies local CSS to hide Shorts-related page elements. This processing happens on your device. No browsing history or video information is recorded.

YouTube continues to operate under its own privacy policy. Navigating to Subscriptions or a video makes normal requests to YouTube, not to a NoShorts service.

Removing the extension stops its behavior. No stored extension data remains because none is created.
